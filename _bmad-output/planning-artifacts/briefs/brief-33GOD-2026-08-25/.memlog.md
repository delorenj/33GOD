- (decision) Keep DeloHQ inside Holocene as the Telegram Mini App; this concept evolves the live surface instead of proposing a replacement product.
- (decision) Frame DeloHQ as the executive interface to an agent company: it answers who is doing what, what needs Jarad, and what changed, rather than exposing raw service administration.
- (decision) Make the living org chart the home and navigation model; every agent has an office that joins identity, current work, recent outcomes, project board, memory, and conversation.
- (decision) Use PJangler-resolved fleet identity and Bloodbank/Candystore activity as product truth; org.yaml is a presentation overlay until Flume owns real authority and reporting structure.
- (decision) Use Telegram conversation for agent interaction and notifications, and the Mini App for shared visual context, approvals, comparison, and company-wide navigation.
- (decision) Every consequential executive action produces a visible receipt and durable audit event; DeloHQ never implies success from a button press alone.
- (assumption) DeloHQ is initially a private, single-operator product for Jarad, optimized for quick mobile checks and interventions throughout the day.
- (assumption) TDLib is deferred until native user-client behavior such as folder synchronization or cross-chat aggregation proves valuable; Bot API plus Mini App is the first delivery surface.
- (change) Drafted the product brief around a before-and-after story, four core surfaces, a day-in-the-life journey, explicit first-slice boundaries, and measurable success signals.
- (change) Separated Telegram-native, TDLib, Momo, Flume, voice, replay, boardroom, delegation, and budget possibilities into addendum.md as exploratory ideas rather than first-release commitments.
- (event) Required structure and prose review completed; moved the day-in-the-life before feature decomposition, added a screen map, and added grounding references.
- (event) Product concept complete and ready for Jarad's review; open assumptions remain intentionally visible.
