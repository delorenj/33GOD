## What the PRD carries forward

- Contextual Telegram notifications and exact deep links are MVP requirements.
- Morning/evening briefs, Company replay, delegation slips, Company Memory,
  voice navigation, Boardroom, Intervention Mode, and TDLib are preserved as
  future direction or explicitly excluded from MVP.
- The idea bank's warning that chat, Telegram folders, raw events, and activity
  volume must not become company authority is represented in Non-Goals and the
  authority boundary.

## Gaps or decisions made during reconciliation

1. The idea bank contains mechanism and platform ideas rather than acceptance
   criteria; the PRD retains only the user-visible commitments needed for the
   first release.
2. The idea bank's Company Memory concept needed a bounded definition; the PRD
   defines it as an approved summary of relevant Hindsight context, never a raw
   memory dump.
3. The idea bank does not define priority among deferred concepts; the PRD
   preserves the concepts without assigning a roadmap order.
