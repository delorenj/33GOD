## What the PRD carries forward

- The product promise: Jarad can understand company posture, attention,
  ownership, and action outcome quickly.
- The four first-slice surfaces: Company, Now, Executive Inbox, and Agent
  Office.
- The day-in-the-life journey, translated into four named user journeys with
  entry state, path, climax, resolution, and edge case.
- The Telegram-first front door, mobile-first experience, deep links, and
  visible progress receipts.
- The principle that DeloHQ is an executive experience rather than a system of
  record.

## Gaps or decisions made during reconciliation

1. The brief names the domain owners but does not define a stable capability
   contract; the PRD adds FR-1 through FR-16 and cross-cutting requirements.
2. The brief describes the desired states but does not define how stale,
   missing, or contradictory evidence is represented; the PRD makes `Unknown`
   and stale states explicit.
3. The brief leaves the DeloHQ-versus-Flume naming question open; the PRD and
   addendum adopt DeloHQ as the executive surface and Flume as workforce
   authority, while deferring a separate repository or rename.
4. The brief describes action receipts conceptually; the PRD makes duplicate
   prevention, correlation, terminal outcomes, and unknown completion
   evidence explicit.

## Qualitative material preserved outside the FR spine

The brief's company-language tone, quiet-success posture, and distinction
between an executive surface and an operator console remain in the Vision,
Information Architecture, and addendum handoff notes.
