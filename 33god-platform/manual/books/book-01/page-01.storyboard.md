## 1. One idea

**You press Enter. Your helper's hook drops a note on the bus. The bus keeps it a week. Candystore reads it from the bus. Nobody phones anybody.**

## 2. Reader outcome and the test

Damian says back after one read:

> "When I press Enter, hook-hub pins a note on the bus. The bus keeps it for about a week. Candystore reads it from there, so hook-hub never calls Candystore."

**5-second glance** (cover the page after 5 seconds, then ask):
1. "What is in the middle?" Good answer: a wall, a thick thing that holds notes.
2. "Do the two sides touch?" Good answer: no.

**One-read tell-back prompt:** "Tell me what happens when you press Enter, as if you were telling Lilian."

**Transfer questions** (the printed page does not answer these, so a correct reply is understanding, not parroting):
1. "Where is the original note now?" Expect: still on the bus. Candystore has its own copy.
2. "Does hook-hub phone Candystore?" Expect: no, not for notes.
3. "If a third thing wanted to hear it, would hook-hub change?" Expect: no.

**Scoring (FAA/Jentsch three-way). Only "wrong" fails.**

| Score | Answer looks like |
|---|---|
| Complete | Names a speaker (hook-hub or "the hook"), says the bus keeps notes about a week, and says Candystore reads from the bus. |
| Incomplete but safe | "Messages go on the bus." "The bus remembers things." |
| Wrong or unsafe | "hook-hub sends it to Candystore." "The bus deletes a note once it is read." "It is gone after a week everywhere." |

n = 1 reader is a fast gate, not validation. Record the verbatim tell-back in [`feedback/LOG.md`](../../feedback/LOG.md).

### What the picture preserves and what it distorts

- Preserves: speakers and listeners never touch each other for notes. The wall is the only thing both touch. The wall keeps notes after they are read. Each listener gets its own copy.
- Distorts, marked: the hook has a small helper not drawn (wink 3, bb-hook). Candystore does not touch the wall directly (wink 7, a helper reads it for the app).
- Distorts, unmarked on this page because it is outside the claim: the wall is drawn as one panel, not as the server that holds many walls (page 4 shows Bloodbank, NATS and the stream).
