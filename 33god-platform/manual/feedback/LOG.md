# Feedback log

Every published page gets read by Damian (n = 1, a fast first gate, not validation). Record what he says. Every grammar change cites a row here.

## How to run a read (5 minutes)

1. Send the page. No introduction beyond "tell me what you see".
2. **5-second glance.** After 5 seconds, ask the two glance questions in the page's storyboard.
3. **One-read tell-back.** Ask the tell-back prompt. Copy the answer verbatim. Do not correct him yet.
4. **Transfer questions.** Ask the page's transfer questions. These test understanding, not recall.
5. Ask where he stopped reading and what he skipped.
6. Score three ways: **complete**, **incomplete but safe**, **wrong or unsafe**. Only "wrong" is a failure.
7. Collect his comments on the page (the comment channel is an open question, see `books/README.md` and the brief).
8. If anything changed in the grammar or the page, fill the last column and bump the grammar version.
