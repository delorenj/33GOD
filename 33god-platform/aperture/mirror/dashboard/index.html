  4. Open Terminal, paste sudo ~/33god-boot-daemon.sh --apply, press Return, and type your Mac password (nothing shows as you type). It makes your Mac start its background apps by itself after a restart, instead of waiting for you to sign in. It checks itself and undoes the change if anything looks wrong. Your apps blink off for a short while as it runs. Heads up: your disk is locked, so after a power cut the Mac still waits for your password first. For a planned restart, ask us for the one command.
