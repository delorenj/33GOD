## Issues

1. **HIGH - the lamp removal truncated `~/.codex/config.toml`, and the evidence does not say so.**
   The worker's removal ran `re.subn(r'(?m)^\[mcp_servers\.lamp\]\s*\n(?:[^\[][^\n]*\n|\s*\n)*', '', raw, count=1)`.
   The negated class `[^\[]` also matches a newline, so after the blank line that ends the lamp block the pattern consumes the next line even when it starts with `[`.
   It then alternates to the end of the file.
   Its own check (`'lamp' not in parsed['mcp_servers']`, "TOML parses") cannot see that.
   I replayed that exact regex on the backup in scratch: 11308 -> 2964 bytes, and the live file is exactly that text plus 1817 bytes of ten `[hooks.state."...hooks.json:..."]` sections (all ten, nothing else) that `reconcile_trust` then rewrote through Codex's `config/batchWrite`.
   A lamp-only removal would have left 11254 bytes.
   The ten hook-trust hashes in the backup are byte-identical to the live ones, so restoring from the backup loses nothing.

   Lost from Damian's live config: 13 `[projects."..."] trust_level = "trusted"` entries (Hermes Setup, Damian's Desk, damian-33god-deploy, Music Lab, flume, voxxy, 33GOD, hello-subconscious-team-inbox, CoachingAgentFramework, hello-subconscious-company, space, `~/code`, `~/HeyMa`); the stale `[hooks.state]` entries, including trust for `damian-33god-deploy/.codex/hooks.json` session_start and stop; `[tui]` (screen reader flag, model NUX, preset 1); `[features]` (`prevent_idle_sleep = true`, `analytics_plan_history`, `login_shell_package_path = true`); and the `[[skills.config]]` blocks that disable about 25 synced skills, which are now enabled again.
