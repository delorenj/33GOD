- **Failure path (not hypothetical):**

  4. No kickstart is needed for this to happen.

  PLAN risk 1 is therefore live now. **Recommendation:** run W7-1's restore-to-durable-path plus plist repair (no restart) as the next 33GOD action on the Mac.
